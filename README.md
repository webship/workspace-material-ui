# @webship.co/workspace-material-ui

Material UI for the [Webship Workspace](https://github.com/webship/workspace) dashboard: a builder that makes an app
with Material UI's own Vite starter (with its free templates and documentation demos, or the starter alone), or takes
one from its repository, builds it and serves it from a container of its own; the
[Material UI step pack](https://github.com/webship/material-ui-steps) wired into the testing setup of that app; and its
AI items for building and testing Material UI apps.

The builder, its functions, its testing hook and its AI items come with the first release, `1.0.0`.
